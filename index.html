Split Money App 💸

Technologies:

Kotlin, Java, Firebase, WebSockets, Room DB

Description:

The Split Money app revolutionizes how you manage shared expenses and track balances within groups. Whether you’re dining out with friends, planning a trip, or sharing household bills, this app ensures everyone pays their fair share without any hassle.

Key Features:

  • Real-Time Notifications: Stay updated with instant notifications, powered by Firebase, so you never miss a payment update.
  • Seamless Communication: Chat with your group members using WebSockets for a smooth and efficient way to discuss expenses and settle balances.
  • Secure and Reliable: All your data is stored securely using Room DB, providing a robust and trustworthy database solution.
  • User-Friendly Interface: Enjoy a sleek, intuitive design that makes tracking and splitting expenses a breeze.

Say goodbye to awkward conversations about money and hello to a smarter way to manage shared expenses with the Split Money app. 🎉
